# About the test plan

`product-catalog.md` is the planner agent's raw, unedited discovery output. It is
evidence of what the agent produced, not an acceptance contract for this small
suite.

The plan intentionally remains untouched, including its over-broad suggestions,
proposed file paths and the incorrect claim that separate checkout/auth plans
exist. During review I selected four distinct mechanisms and implemented the
smallest representative scenario for each. The selection rationale is in
[`../docs/trade-offs.md`](../docs/trade-offs.md).
